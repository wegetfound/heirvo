About this memory
A muggy July afternoon in the backyard of the Bedford house. Mom planned it for months. Uncle Jerry brought the wrong kind of charcoal. Dad pretended to be surprised and we all pretended to believe him. Toward sundown, Helen gave a toast that nobody got through with dry eyes — including the cameraman, judging by the gentle wobble around minute 43.
