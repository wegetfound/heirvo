## Email 4: Support Email (Error in Recovery)

**Subject:** We're here to help — your Heirvo recovery issue

**From:** Heirvo <info@heirvo.com>

---

### Email Body

Hi [USER_NAME],

Thanks for reaching out about your recovery issue. We're sorry you hit a snag.

**Your issue:** [ERROR_MESSAGE]

---

### Let's get your disc back:

Here are some things to try:

1. **Clean your disc.** Use a soft microfiber cloth and wipe gently from the center out (not in circles). Dust and scratches block the laser.

2. **Try Patient mode.** If you used Normal mode, try Patient mode instead. It reads slower and more gently — great for discs that other software gives up on.

3. **Check your USB drive.** For Patient mode, use a **powered** USB drive (not bus-powered). Bus-powered drives can disconnect during long recovery sessions.

4. **Try a different USB port.** Sometimes a port acts flaky. Switching ports solves it.

5. **Restart Heirvo.** Close the app completely and open it again.

---

### Still stuck?

Tell us:
- What's the exact error message?
- What type of disc (DVD, CD, photo CD, etc.)?
- Have you cleaned the disc?
- Are you using Patient mode?

Reply to this email with those details and we'll dig deeper.
