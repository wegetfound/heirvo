### 1. Independent contractor status

You are an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, partner, or franchisee of Heirvo.
This Agreement creates no employment or franchise relationship. You control your own hours,
methods, and workspace. Heirvo specifies quality outcomes and handling standards — not your
minute-to-minute work process.

You are responsible for your own taxes (including self-employment tax). Heirvo will issue a
Form 1099-NEC as required and will not withhold taxes on your behalf.

You are free to provide similar disc-recovery or data-recovery services to other clients,
provided you have no active job assigned under this Agreement that requires your immediate
attention and that you do not use Heirvo's brand, marks, or software for third-party work.

### 2. Equipment and workspace

You will obtain your own equipment — optical drives, cleaning supplies, shipping materials —
from **independent third-party sellers at market prices**. Heirvo does not sell, supply, or
finance equipment and receives no commission from any vendor. You own your equipment outright.

Heirvo specifies minimum capability standards (compatible Pioneer and LiteOn drives; a
dedicated, lockable workspace). The choice of vendor and the secondhand/new decision is yours.

### 3. Services

You will perform disc-recovery jobs assigned to you via Heirvo's routing system in accordance
with the then-current Operator Handbook. The Handbook sets quality specifications and
chain-of-custody protocols — it is not employment-style supervision.

You **may decline any job** without penalty. Declining jobs does not affect your standing
unless you are consistently unavailable during periods when you have indicated availability.

Heirvo performs quality control before any files are delivered to a customer.

### 4. Chain of custody and handling

**Originals are irreplaceable.** You will follow the chain-of-custody protocol for every job:
photograph condition on arrival, log each handling step, and use insured, tracked shipping on
every leg. Deviating from protocol — not a carrier loss you had no part in — is the
accountability trigger below.

If you follow the documented protocol and a loss or damage occurs anyway (e.g., carrier
negligence), Heirvo bears the customer-facing responsibility. If loss or damage results from
**your failure to follow the protocol or from your own negligence**, it is grounds for the
strikes or immediate termination in §7.

### 5. Confidentiality and customer data

Customer media, data, and personal information are strictly confidential. Use them only to
perform the assigned job. Do not copy, retain, examine beyond what recovery requires, or
disclose them. Securely delete all customer data after confirmed delivery, within
`[RETENTION WINDOW]` days.

Do not permit any unauthorized person to access customer media or your workspace during a job.
This obligation survives termination of the Agreement.

### 6. Designated service area

Heirvo may designate a metropolitan service area for you. The area is **granted and earned,
never purchased** — no fee or deposit is charged. It is not a property right; it is held only
while you meet the standards in §7 and may be modified or revoked as provided there.

Heirvo may route overflow jobs to other operators when you are at or beyond capacity. A
designated area does not guarantee any volume of work or minimum income.

### 7. Standards, strikes, and termination

**Immediate termination** (service area revoked at once, software access ended) for:
dishonesty of any kind (concealing damage, falsifying logs or recovery results); loss or
destruction of an original through protocol breach; allowing unauthorized access to customer
media or your workspace; retaining or disclosing customer data; or a disqualifying criminal
background event discovered after acceptance.

**Escalating strikes** for quality and SLA issues (not integrity breaches):
Strike 1 — coaching conversation; Strike 2 — probation with 100% QC review;
Strike 3 — service area revocation. Three quality strikes in any rolling
`[WINDOW, e.g., 90-day]` period ends the relationship.

Either party may terminate this Agreement on `[NOTICE PERIOD, e.g., 14 days]` written notice.
Heirvo may terminate immediately for §7 integrity events. On any termination, you will complete
or hand off in-flight jobs per Heirvo's instructions, return or destroy Heirvo materials, and
cease using Heirvo's name, marks, and software. §§5 and 8 survive termination.

### 8. Compensation

You are paid per-job at the rates in the then-current pay schedule (Tier 1–3 piece rates,
attempt fee for genuine no-recovery attempts, and a monthly quality bonus for meeting QC and
on-time thresholds). Rates are set by Heirvo and may be updated on `[NOTICE PERIOD]` advance
notice. No payment is owed for work that violates this Agreement or for falsified results.

Payment is made `[via Stripe Connect / ACH / method]` on `[schedule, e.g., net-15 from invoice]`.
You invoice Heirvo; Heirvo does not initiate payment automatically.

### 9. Branding and software license

Heirvo grants you a limited, non-transferable, revocable license to use the Heirvo name,
marks, and Lab Edition software solely to perform jobs under this Agreement. All Heirvo
intellectual property remains Heirvo's. The license ends on termination.

## Counsel checklist

1. **IC classification** — stress-test §1 against the ABC test (prong B especially: recovery is
   Heirvo's core business) and your state's control test. Confirm "outcomes not process" framing
   and right-to-decline language are sufficient, or advise structural changes.
2. **FTC Franchise Rule** — confirm no-fee + operator-owned equipment structure stays outside
   the franchise definition. Check franchise-registration states if expanding beyond home state.
3. **Liability split (§4)** — set insurance minimums, liability caps, and make the
   protocol-based split enforceable. Align with customer-facing goodwill policy.
4. **Data/privacy (§5)** — add full NDA, data-processing terms, breach-notification, retention
   schedule, and any state privacy-law obligations given customer PII and irreplaceable media.
5. **Background checks** — FCRA compliance (disclosure and authorization) for the §7
   post-acceptance background event clause.
6. **Payments (§8)** — 1099-NEC mechanics, Stripe Connect terms, state wage-law risk if
   classification is challenged.
