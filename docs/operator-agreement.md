# Heirvo Lab Operator Agreement — Template (DRAFT)

> ⚠️ **THIS IS A DRAFT TEMPLATE, NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** It encodes the business structure designed
> in [lab-network.md](lab-network.md) into contract-shaped language so a qualified attorney has
> a concrete starting point. **A franchise/employment attorney MUST review and finalize it
> before it is used.** Two areas are existential and lawyer-gated: (1) staying outside the FTC

## Heirvo Lab Operator Agreement

This Independent Lab Operator Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of `[DATE]` between
`[HEIRVO LEGAL ENTITY]` ("Heirvo," "we," "us") and `[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]` ("Operator," "you").

### 1. Nature of the relationship

1.1 You are an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer
of Heirvo. Nothing here creates an employment or franchise relationship.

1.2 This Agreement is **not a franchise.** No franchise fee, deposit, or other required payment
is charged by Heirvo as a condition of this Agreement, now or within the first six months or
thereafter.
*[Why: defeats element (c) of the FTC Franchise Rule — brand + control are present, so the
$500-payment element must be absent. lab-network.md §11.]*

1.3 You control the manner and means of your work — your own hours, your own methods, your own
equipment — and you may provide similar services to others. Heirvo specifies **outcomes and
quality standards**, not the minute-to-minute manner of performance.
*[Why: supports independent-contractor classification under control-based and ABC tests; manage
outcomes, not process. lab-network.md §11.]*

1.4 You are responsible for your own taxes, including self-employment tax; Heirvo will issue an
IRS Form 1099 as required and will not withhold taxes.

### 2. License to use the Heirvo brand and software

2.1 Heirvo grants you a **limited, non-transferable, revocable** license to use the Heirvo name,
marks, and Lab Edition software **solely** to perform recovery jobs assigned under this
Agreement, subject to the brand and quality standards Heirvo provides.

2.2 The license confers no ownership. All Heirvo software, marks, documentation, and the Lab
Network system remain Heirvo's exclusive property. You acquire no goodwill in the marks.

2.3 You will run only the genuine, current Lab Edition build and will not modify, reverse
engineer, or tamper with it or its telemetry (see §7).

### 3. Territory

3.1 Heirvo may **grant** you a designated metropolitan territory. The territory is **granted
and earned, never sold or purchased.** No fee or deposit is paid for it.
*[Why: "granted not sold" both reinforces the no-franchise structure and avoids creating a
purchased asset the Operator could later claim. lab-network.md §11–12.]*

3.2 The territory is a **revocable performance lease, not property.** You hold it only while you
meet the standards in §6 and §8. It may be modified or revoked as provided in §9.

3.3 Heirvo intends to maintain one customer-facing operator per territory but may **route
overflow** to other operators when you are at or beyond capacity, and may reassign in-flight
jobs if you become unresponsive. Territory does not guarantee any volume of work or income.

### 4. Equipment

4.1 You will obtain your own equipment (optical drives, cleaning supplies, shipping materials)
**from independent third-party sellers at market prices.** Heirvo does **not** sell, supply,
finance, or mark up equipment, and receives no commission or referral fee from any vendor.
*[Why: critical FTC guardrail — operational equipment bought from the franchisor/affiliate
counts toward the $500 franchise threshold; equipment bought on the open market does not.
lab-network.md §11.]*

4.2 You own your equipment. Heirvo specifies only minimum capability standards (e.g., a
compatible Pioneer and LiteOn drive), not a mandated vendor.

### 5. The work and quality standards

5.1 You will perform recovery jobs in accordance with the then-current Operator Handbook, which
sets **quality specifications and protocols** (not employment-style supervision).

5.2 You will meet the service levels (turnaround, quality) communicated for each job. Heirvo
performs quality control before any files are delivered to a customer.

5.3 **Originals are sacred.** You will follow the chain-of-custody and handling protocols,
including imaging an original before recovery attempts wherever feasible.

### 6. Chain of custody, handling, and liability

6.1 You will follow the mandatory, logged chain-of-custody protocol for every job: documented
condition on arrival (photographs), per-step logging, and insured, tracked shipping on every
leg.

6.2 **Protocol-based liability.** If you follow the documented chain-of-custody protocol and a
loss or damage nonetheless occurs (e.g., a carrier loss), Heirvo will bear the resulting
customer-facing responsibility. If a loss or damage results from your **failure to follow the
protocol** or from your negligence, you are accountable, and it is grounds for the strikes or
termination in §9.
*[Why: pure Heirvo-absorbs breeds carelessness; pure operator-liable poisons recruiting.
Protocol-based threading is the designed middle. The Operator's territory/standing — not
unlimited personal liability — is the primary consequence. lab-network.md §8. Counsel to set
any liability caps/insurance requirements.]*

6.3 `[Counsel: insert any required shipping insurance minimums, liability caps, and whether
Operator must carry their own insurance.]`

### 7. Telemetry, monitoring, and audit

7.1 You consent to Lab Edition generating and transmitting **signed recovery telemetry** for
each job, and to Heirvo's quality-control sampling, **blind re-test jobs**, and audits.
*[Why: the telemetry is the backbone of pay and anti-cheat. lab-telemetry-spec.md.]*

7.2 You will not falsify, alter, spoof, or circumvent telemetry, custody logs, or recovery
results. Doing so is a material breach and grounds for immediate termination (§9).

7.3 You consent to the secure handling of job data through Heirvo's systems for the purpose of
routing, QC, payment, and liability records.

### 8. Confidentiality and customer data

8.1 Customer media and data are strictly confidential. You will use them **only** to perform
the assigned job, will not copy, retain, or disclose them, and will **securely delete** all
customer data after delivery and any required retention window.

8.2 You will not allow any unauthorized person to access customer media, your workspace, or
Heirvo systems.

8.3 This obligation survives termination. `[Counsel: full NDA / data-processing terms,
breach-notification, and any applicable privacy-law obligations.]`

### 9. Standards of conduct, strikes, and removal

9.1 **Immediate termination** (territory revoked at once) for: dishonesty of any kind
(concealing damage/loss, faking logs, falsifying results); loss or destruction of an original
through protocol breach; working outside your secured workspace or permitting unauthorized
access; exfiltrating or retaining customer data; or a disqualifying background event.

9.2 **Escalating strikes** for quality/SLA issues (not integrity): Strike 1 — coaching; Strike
2 — probation with elevated QC at your cost; Strike 3 — territory revocation. Three quality
strikes in a rolling window, or a quality score below the network floor, ends the territory.
*[Why: mirrors lab-network.md §6 exactly.]*

9.3 Heirvo may revoke your software license and Lab Edition keys upon termination; you will
cease using the marks and software and return or destroy Heirvo materials.

### 10. Compensation

10.1 You are paid per the then-current pay schedule: tiered per-disc rates set automatically by
the recovery telemetry (not self-report), an attempt fee for QC-confirmed genuine attempts on
non-recoverable discs, and a monthly quality bonus for meeting QC and on-time thresholds.
*[Why: lab-network.md §7; tiers derived from telemetry per lab-telemetry-spec.md §7.]*

10.2 Payment terms, schedule, and method `[e.g., via Stripe Connect]` are as set out in
`[SCHEDULE A / the operator dashboard]`. Heirvo may update the pay schedule prospectively on
`[NOTICE PERIOD]` notice.

10.3 No payment is owed for work that violates this Agreement or for falsified results.

### 11. Term and termination

11.1 This Agreement begins on acceptance and continues until terminated. Either party may
terminate `[for convenience on NOTICE]`; Heirvo may terminate immediately for the §9.1 events.

11.2 On termination: license and territory end, keys are revoked, you complete or hand off
in-flight jobs per Heirvo's instructions (recall/forward protocol), and §§7.2, 8, 12 survive.

## Counsel checklist (the must-confirm list)

1. **FTC Franchise Rule** — confirm the no-fee / no-equipment-from-Heirvo / paid-trial structure
   keeps this outside the franchise definition in all operating states; check franchise-
   registration states specifically.
2. **Worker classification** — stress-test against the ABC test (esp. prong B, since recovery is
   Heirvo's business) and federal/state control tests; confirm the manage-outcomes-not-process
   language and gig terms are sufficient, or advise structural changes.
3. **Liability allocation (§6)** — set insurance minimums, caps, and the protocol-based split in
   enforceable terms; align with the customer-facing goodwill policy.
4. **Data/privacy (§8)** — full NDA + data-processing + breach-notification + retention terms;
   any state privacy-law exposure given customer PII and irreplaceable media.
5. **Background checks** — FCRA compliance (disclosure/authorization) for the §9.1 / acceptance
   background-check requirement.
6. **Payments** — 1099 mechanics; Stripe Connect terms; any state wage-law risk if
   classification is challenged.
