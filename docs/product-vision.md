# Product Vision

**One sentence:** Heirvo is a Windows app that rescues irreplaceable memories from damaged optical media — DVDs, photo CDs, and audio CDs — and turns them into a permanent personal media vault, built for people who are scared, not technical.

## Who it's for

A 45–65-year-old who just discovered their wedding/childhood/parent-funeral DVD won't play, or found a box of old Kodak Photo CDs they can't open. They are emotional, not technical, and one bad UX away from giving up.

## The promise

> Even if your disc is too damaged to play, we'll recover everything we can — and you'll never lose progress. Your memories live on your device, yours forever.

## Three rules that override every other decision

1. **Never fail the whole job because of one unreadable sector.** Always produce a playable output, even if partial.
2. **Always be resumable.** Pulling the disc, crashing, rebooting — recovery picks up exactly where it left off, including merging reads from different drives or sessions.
3. **Speak human.** No "UDF", "ISO9660", "VOB", "IFO", "sector LBA". Translate everything. *"We found 47 minutes of video across 3 chapters."*

## What Heirvo does

- **Disc recovery** — multi-pass sector recovery for DVDs, photo CDs (Kodak PCD), audio CDs. The core, irreplaceable feature.
- **Personal media vault** — import photos, videos, and audio from folders. All media in one managed library, originals never touched.
- **Whisper transcription** — local offline transcription. Search by what was said, not just file names.
- **EXIF-aware organisation** — photos land in the right year automatically.
- **Labs network** — home-based operators in 9 US metros handling mail-in disc recovery for customers who can't do it themselves.

## Anti-goals

- Not a Blu-ray ripper. Not a piracy tool.
- Not a video editor.
- Not cross-platform v1. Windows only.
- Not cloud. 100% local processing is a feature, not a limitation.
- Not subscription. One-time license.
